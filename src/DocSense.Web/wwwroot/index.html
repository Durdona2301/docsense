How DocSense finds answers

Documents are split into overlapping passages and stored in your local PostgreSQL database. Extractive mode finds keywords and shows original text. With Ollama enabled, DocSense also searches embeddings and generates answers with source references.

Questions are independent; previous answers are not sent as chat history. Model answers can be wrong: always inspect their sources. Scanned PDFs need OCR before upload.
